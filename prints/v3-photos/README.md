# V3 working photos, 2026-09-25

Taken by Austin Griffith of our own prints and his own hardware, on his bench,
and handed to the V3 design session on 2026-09-25 "for the record books".
Austin's own photos of this repo's own parts; released under the repo's MIT
license. No third-party case, mesh or image is in any frame.

These are **record photos, not measurements.** No dimension in `cad/` or
`MEASUREMENTS.md` comes from them. The caliper and steel rule in IMG_0817 are
bench tools in the background and show no reading used anywhere.
