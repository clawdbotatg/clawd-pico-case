## Result — socket full of strings (IMG_0887)

The photo shows cap B (2 notches) from underneath, with loops of loose
filament inside the socket. Cause, owned by Claude's design: printed flange
down, the socket has two flat features in mid-air. There is a ledge where the
3.0 round mouth narrows to the square, and a flat roof over the square. The
printer has to bridge both over nothing, and PETG strings and droops there.
J2 has the same ledge and roof, so a clean J2 was partly luck. It is not a
printer fault. The next socket must be self-supporting (45° transitions, no
flat overhangs), and that needs the real stem shape measured.
