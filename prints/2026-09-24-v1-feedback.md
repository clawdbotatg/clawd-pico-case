## Requested changes

1. **Joystick:** mount on the board before the lid. Retaining lip inside;
   shaft and smaller round ball protrude. Taller, less wide and flat.
2. **Opening:** small pry notch at the seam; case is too hard to take apart.
3. **USB-C:** connector sits higher than the hole. Shift opening toward
   lid and reduce excess space while preserving cable access. Photo shows
   extra space below the socket but provides no reliable millimetre offset.
   Check the actual printed revision/stack; do not overwrite caliper data
   with a perspective-photo estimate.
4. **Buttons:** wider rectangles, not squares; taller above the lid.
   Reduce top/bottom flange lips while retaining caps. Rectangular holes
   should make insertion orientation clear. Exact sizes remain design
   choices to check against button pitch, retention and travel.

Keep other features unless required clearance checks show a problem.

5. **Bottom button access:** Austin requests a hole directly over the pink
   board's button/pad, about its size, for a toothpick or thin tool without
   opening the case. Button identity (reset versus boot selection), centre
   position and pad dimensions must be confirmed from this actual board.
   Do not infer reset behaviour from the word Pico. Prefer a nonconductive
   tool; keep opening and tool path clear of adjacent components.
