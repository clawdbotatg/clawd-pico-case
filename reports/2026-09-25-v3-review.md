# V3 — browser review, not approved for printing

Built from this repository's original MIT geometry and Austin's feedback.
No external case models. No printer upload or print request for V3.

## Changes to inspect

- **Flat lid, no joystick tower.** Entire top is 4.0 mm higher than V1/V2's
  flat surface, as allowed by Austin. Case body is31.44 ×57.50 ×29.24 mm;
  joystick-inclusive height34.74. Screen sits deeper in the opening.
- **Earlier joystick interface.** Restore2.01 square socket, roof z5.30,
  bottom z3.40 and Ø5 lower neck from original full-case commit4010773.
  V2 had narrowed the socket to1.91. Exact physical V1 cap identity is not
  independently confirmed; the earlier lid-only cap also used2.01/5.30,
  but differed in neck diameter and lower height. This assumption is explicit.
- **V2-sized ball with hidden retention.** Ø7 ball on shaft, Ø10.4 lip
  inside, Ø9.6 lid opening. Lid passes over ball but captures lip. Underside
  pocket and tapered lip provide movement space without an exterior collar.
- **No support layer intended.** Lid prints flat face-down. Joystick prints
  upside down, like earlier caps: a0.8-deep flat on the ball gives bed contact;
  a45-degree taper above the retaining lip avoids a broad unsupported flange.
  Ball is rounded but not a complete sphere. Inspect this visible flat.
- **Closed USB hole, no lid tab.** Complete opening belongs to base again.
  V2 alignment and cable recess retained; Austin reports alignment good.
- Keep successful pry notches, bottom button access and rectangular caps.
  Button stems are4 mm longer, retaining1.8 mm exposed above the higher lid.

## Browser controls

Open `renders/viewer.html`: drag to rotate, wheel/pinch to zoom, shift-drag
to pan. Use Explode, part checkboxes, transparency and preset views to
inspect the hidden lip, closed USB port and bottom hole. Browser smoke test
checks rendered triangles, orbit, presets, explode, transparency and toggles.
Internet is needed for the existing three.js CDN dependency.

## Checks and limits

Executed result: 206 required geometry checks passed. Lid bed contact is
912.1 mm². All seven print/sample meshes have bed z=0; exporter checks closed
edges and removes duplicate-vertex facets. Manifest source/output hashes
match. Browser test passed with no JavaScript errors and verified controls.
Separately, 48 assumed joystick-body motion scenarios intersect the guessed
hardware body; these remain unresolved, not counted as passing checks.

- Geometry audit requires solid validity, flat lid bed contact, no raised
  collar/no fin, exact earlier lower joystick shape and socket parameters,
  nominal assembly, button travel/retention, cap capture and motion against
  the lid, and installation of lid over mounted ball.
- Separate Pico has a sampled collision-free USB-first path: reverse it by
  tipping12 degrees around USB front, withdrawing1.8 mm and lifting out.
  This is not proof of every intermediate pose or real solder clearance.
  Insert Pico first and then attach hat; no straight drop of connected stack.
- Original lower neck is deliberately restored rather than shortened to
  satisfy a guessed joystick-body model. Some assumed tilt/press scenarios
  intersect that guessed body; each is retained in validation.json as an
  unresolved diagnostic. Actual pivot/body/travel need physical confirmation.
- Lip capture leaves little engagement near the upward stop; test gently.
  Screen-side pocket wall is0.4 mm at its narrowest; inspect slicer continuity.
- **Support-free intent is not slicer or print validation.** No toolpath has
  been sliced for V3. USB roof and latch slots require short bridges; inspect
  them, ball first layers, socket and thin pocket wall before any future print.
- Blue flag identity, omitted components/solder and stack datum remain
  unresolved. Bottom hole is user-confirmed aligned, but button function is
  not established by geometry. No new measurements are needed just to review
  this draft; physical fit still needs testing after approval.
